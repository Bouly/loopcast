# Illustrations de Loopcast : prompts pour Astra

Le style vient des deux images de référence envoyées le 29/10/2026 : des personnages simples au trait d'encre
noir, posés dans des décors peints (gouache, pastel gras), avec une lumière chaude et forte. On ne les met pas
partout, seulement aux moments où l'écran respire : un état vide, une erreur, une réussite.

## Mode d'emploi

1. Générer d'abord la **planche des personnages** (prompt 0) et garder la meilleure.
2. Pour chaque scène, donner à Astra en référence la planche des personnages et les deux images de style, puis
   coller le prompt complet de la scène. Chaque prompt se suffit à lui-même : rien à assembler.
3. Livrer en PNG, au moins 2048 px de large, au format indiqué. Pas de texte dans l'image : les phrases restent
   dans l'interface.

Les personnages : **Loop**, un raton laveur bricoleur, et **Cast**, une chouette harfang calme et un peu
sceptique (Loop + Cast = Loopcast). Le seul détail vert est la lueur des écrans d'iPhone, le vert « signal » de
l'interface (#C9F24B) ; tout le reste est chaud. Si vous préférez garder le chien et le chat des images de
référence, remplacez le paragraphe « Characters » par celui de la fin de ce fichier ; attention, ces dessins
rappellent ceux de Treats.

## 0. Planche des personnages

```text
Character turnaround sheet for a children's picture book, on plain warm off-white paper (#F3F4F1), no environment.

Characters: Loop, a small raccoon with a white body, a black eye mask drawn as one single band, small round black ears and a fluffy tail with three black rings; curious, busy, always tinkering. Cast, a small snowy owl with a round white body, two tiny ear tufts, big round dot eyes, a short orange beak and a few black feather strokes on the wing tips; calm and slightly skeptical.

Show each character from the front, in three-quarter view and from the back, plus two expressions each: delighted, and unimpressed side-eye. Same scale for both, arranged in two clean rows with generous space.

Style: thin, slightly wobbly black ink line of even weight, hand-drawn; flat warm off-white bodies with soft dry-brush shading and a few small color patches; minimal faces (dot eyes, tiny nose, one short line for the mouth); expressive body language. Looks drawn by hand with ink and gouache, not digital.

Avoid: 3D render, CGI, Pixar or Disney style, anime, chibi, flat vector art, glossy plastic look, neon, gradients, glow, text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, watermark, signature, extra limbs, photorealism.
```

## 1. Le travail est fait (Validation, rien à valider)

```text
Wide cinematic picture-book illustration, 16:9.

Scene: seen from behind, Loop the raccoon and Cast the owl sit side by side on a grassy hill at sunset, looking at a calm sea with distant islands and a low sun. Between them an iPhone lies face up in the grass, its screen a soft, plain lime-green glow (#C9F24B) with nothing on it. Tall painterly grass in the foreground, a huge warm sky taking two thirds of the frame. Mood: the work is done, peaceful and tender.

Characters: Loop, a small raccoon with a white body, a black eye mask drawn as one single band, small round black ears and a fluffy tail with three black rings. Cast, a small snowy owl with a round white body, two tiny ear tufts, big round dot eyes, a short orange beak and a few black feather strokes on the wing tips. Same proportions and line in every image.

Style: mixed media. The characters are simple cartoon figures drawn with a thin, slightly wobbly black ink line, flat warm off-white bodies with soft dry-brush shading and minimal faces. The environment is richly painted in gouache and oil pastel with visible, layered brush strokes and no outlines, atmospheric depth, strong golden-hour light and soft deep shadows. Palette: burnt orange, amber, apricot, warm cream, charcoal; the only green is the phone glow. Composition off-center, lots of negative space.

Avoid: 3D render, CGI, Pixar or Disney style, anime, flat vector art, glossy look, neon, purple or blue gradients, lens flare, bokeh, glowing aura around the characters, any text, letters, numbers, logos or app interface on the phone screen, watermark, signature, extra limbs, photorealism, clutter, centered symmetrical composition.
```

## Variante : le chien et le chat des images de référence

Remplacer le paragraphe « Characters » de chaque prompt par :

```text
Characters: a white dog with long floppy black ears, a black nose, a small orange patch on its back and an orange collar; enthusiastic and curious. A white cat with orange patches on the ears, back and tail, thin whisker lines, a little grumpy and skeptical. Same proportions and line in every image.
```

Et dans les scènes, lire « the dog » pour Loop et « the cat » pour Cast.
